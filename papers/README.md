| *A new upper bound for the density of planar sets avoiding unit distances* (5 pages). m₁(ℝ²) ≤ 0.24353 (the published bound was 0.2470, Ambrus, Csiszárik, Matolcsi, Varga and Zsámboki, 2024): their inclusion–exclusion linear program for their 23 points and eight more points of ℚ(√3, √11), with a rational certificate checked by two programs written separately. Hence χ_f(ℝ²) ≥ 4.1063. The same group announced 0.2415 in 2023 without a published proof; this is cited. One internal referee (correct; one checker hardened). | draft, 11 October 2026 | [PDF](unit-distance-density/unit-distance-density.pdf) · [LaTeX](unit-distance-density/unit-distance-density.tex) · [README](unit-distance-density/README.md) |
| *Measurable density bounds hold for all colourings of the plane with algebraic forbidden distances* (18 pages). OpenAI's transfer from proper to weak measurable colourings works for every set of algebraic forbidden distances and for fractional colourings, so χ(ℝ², D) ≥ 1/m_D(ℝ²). Consequences: χ_f(ℝ²) = 1/m₁(ℝ²), between 4.0485 and 4.36; the least independence ratio of n-point unit-distance graphs tends to m₁(ℝ²); χ(ℝ², {1, 2}) ≥ 7, and {1, 1000} needs 13 colours; uniformly, χ(ℝ², {1, d}) ≥ 7 for every algebraic d ≥ 5.82, ≥ 14 for d ≥ 25 700, ≥ 15 for d ≥ 5·10⁵ and ≥ 17 for d ≥ 8·10⁸; {1, 30, 900, 27000} needs 48; k integer distances can force more than 4.0485^k colours, so Erdős's L₂(r) grows exponentially. Two scales: χ(ℝ², D₁ ∪ N·D₂) ≥ χ_c(ℝ², D₂) for large N, with c = χ(ℝ², D₁), so χ(ℝ², {1, N}) ≥ 25 for large algebraic N although χ_f < 19.01, and ≥ 18 for every algebraic N ≥ 10²⁵. The transfer is formalized in Lean; the density bounds are certified linear programs. Dúcz–Varga (χ_f > 4.0007) and Davies (dimension ≥ 4) are credited. | draft, version 8, 11 October 2026 | [PDF](forbidden-distances/forbidden-distances.pdf) · [LaTeX](forbidden-distances/forbidden-distances.tex) · [README](forbidden-distances/README.md) |
| *The ruler-and-compass plane is not 5-colourable* (6 pages). OpenAI's proof that χ(ℝ²) ≥ 6 works over the constructible numbers, so the points constructible with ruler and compass cannot be 5-coloured, and some finite unit-distance graph with constructible vertices has no proper 5-colouring. Two changes make square roots enough: a lemma on elements of order 2, and a triple average at the exponents −1, 0, 1. The proof is OpenAI's; the modified formal proof is checked in Lean 4. On paper: the plane over every Euclidean field is not 5-colourable, and OpenAI's rigidity theorem fails over the Pythagorean closure of ℚ. | draft, 9 October 2026 | [PDF](ruler-compass/ruler-compass.pdf) · [LaTeX](ruler-compass/ruler-compass.tex) · [README](ruler-compass/README.md) |
| *A short proof that the planes over ℚ(√3, √11) and ℚ(√2, √3) are 4-chromatic* (5 pages). Proves χ(ℚ(√2, √3)²) = 4 and gives a short proof of K. G. Fischer's theorem χ(ℚ(√3, √11)²) = 4 (1994); both are also proved in Lean 4. | 7, 9 October 2026 | [PDF](planes-4-chromatic/planes-4-chromatic.pdf) · [LaTeX](planes-4-chromatic/planes-4-chromatic.tex) · [README](planes-4-chromatic/README.md) |
| *Two-colourable planes over number fields* (5 pages). χ(F²) = 2 if and only if some prime of F above 2 ramifies in F(i), for every number field F (the "if" direction is Theorem A′ of hn-2adic-obstruction; the converse is new, as far as we know); a local–global question, whose four-colour case would give a triangle-free 5-chromatic unit-distance graph in the plane. | draft, 3 October 2026 | [PDF](two-colour-planes/two-colour-planes.pdf) · [LaTeX](two-colour-planes/two-colour-planes.tex) · [README](two-colour-planes/README.md) |
| *Colouring the p-adic plane* (7 pages). For p ≡ 3 (mod 4) every measurable colouring of ℚ_p² needs at least 1 + (p + 1)/(2√p) colours, which answers Question 1 of Bardestani and Mallahi-Karai in the negative; χ(ℚ₇²) = 4, and χ(ℚ₂²) = 2, χ(ℚ₃²) = 3 from Madore's results (all three also in Lean 4); χ(ℚ_p²) = ∞ for p ≡ 1 (mod 4), by a theorem of Davies; χ(ℚ_p²) ≥ 4 for every prime p ≡ 3 (mod 4) with 7 ≤ p < 2 129 503 819. | draft, 2 October 2026 | [PDF](padic-planes/padic-planes.pdf) · [LaTeX](padic-planes/padic-planes.tex) · [README](padic-planes/README.md) |
| *Three colours for the plane over a number field* (26 pages). χ(F²) ≤ 3 if and only if some prime above 2 ramifies in F(i) or some prime above 3 has residue degree 1 (Theorem B), for every number field F; no circular chromatic number strictly between 2 and 3; χ(K²) = 2, 3 or ≥ 4 for the local fields; χ_c(ℚ(√11)²) = χ_c(ℚ(√35)²) = χ_c(ℚ₇²) = 7/2 while χ = 4 (Theorem C, computer proof of the lower bounds); below 4, the best bound from colourings locally constant at one place is 2, 3 or 7/2, and these are the only values below 4: χ_c(F²) is 2, 3, 7/2 or at least 4, with 7/2 exactly when χ ≥ 4 and a prime above 7 has residue degree 1 (Theorems E and F, one computer-assisted step each); by hand, χ_c(F²) ≥ 56/17 when χ(F²) ≥ 4 (Theorem D). One internal referee for the paper, two for Theorem B, two for Theorem D, one each for Theorems E and F. | draft, 4 October 2026 | [PDF](three-colours/three-colours.pdf) · [LaTeX](three-colours/three-colours.tex) · [README](three-colours/README.md) |
| *Which real quadratic planes need four colours* (6 pages). χ(ℚ(√d)²) ≥ 4 for every d ≡ 11 (mod 12), so a real quadratic plane needs four colours exactly when d ≡ 11 (mod 12); χ(ℚ(√d)²) = 4 for all these d except possibly d ≡ 47, 143, 167 (mod 168). A proof by hand from Theorem W: one or two unit vectors rotated by the rational rotations with denominators dividing 5^k have no character into [1/3, 2/3]. Two internal referees. | draft, 3 October 2026 | [PDF](four-colours/four-colours.pdf) · [LaTeX](four-colours/four-colours.tex) · [README](four-colours/README.md) |

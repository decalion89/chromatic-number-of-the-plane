# Papers

Each folder holds one paper: its LaTeX source, the PDF, and a README with the build command and the
files of the repository behind it. None of the papers has been refereed.

| *A short proof that the planes over ℚ(√3, √11) and ℚ(√2, √3) are 4-chromatic* (5 pages). Proves χ(ℚ(√2, √3)²) = 4 and gives a short proof of K. G. Fischer's theorem χ(ℚ(√3, √11)²) = 4 (1994); both are also proved in Lean 4. | 5, 27 September 2026 | [PDF](planes-4-chromatic/planes-4-chromatic.pdf) · [LaTeX](planes-4-chromatic/planes-4-chromatic.tex) · [README](planes-4-chromatic/README.md) |
| *Two-colourable planes over number fields* (5 pages). χ(F²) = 2 if and only if some prime of F above 2 ramifies in F(i), for every number field F (the "if" direction is Theorem A′ of hn-2adic-obstruction; the converse is new, as far as we know); a local–global question, whose four-colour case would give a triangle-free 5-chromatic unit-distance graph in the plane. | draft, 3 October 2026 | [PDF](two-colour-planes/two-colour-planes.pdf) · [LaTeX](two-colour-planes/two-colour-planes.tex) · [README](two-colour-planes/README.md) |
| *Colouring the p-adic plane* (7 pages). For p ≡ 3 (mod 4) every measurable colouring of ℚ_p² needs at least 1 + (p + 1)/(2√p) colours, which answers Question 1 of Bardestani and Mallahi-Karai in the negative; χ(ℚ₇²) = 4, and χ(ℚ₂²) = 2, χ(ℚ₃²) = 3 from Madore's results (all three also in Lean 4); χ(ℚ_p²) = ∞ for p ≡ 1 (mod 4), by a theorem of Davies; χ(ℚ_p²) ≥ 4 for every prime p ≡ 3 (mod 4) with 7 ≤ p < 2 129 503 819. | draft, 2 October 2026 | [PDF](padic-planes/padic-planes.pdf) · [LaTeX](padic-planes/padic-planes.tex) · [README](padic-planes/README.md) |
| *Which real quadratic planes need four colours* (6 pages). χ(ℚ(√d)²) ≥ 4 for every d ≡ 11 (mod 12), so a real quadratic plane needs four colours exactly when d ≡ 11 (mod 12); χ(ℚ(√d)²) = 4 for all these d except possibly d ≡ 47, 143, 167 (mod 168). A proof by hand from Theorem W: one or two unit vectors rotated by the rational rotations with denominators dividing 5^k have no character into [1/3, 2/3]. Two internal referees. | draft, 3 October 2026 | [PDF](four-colours/four-colours.pdf) · [LaTeX](four-colours/four-colours.tex) · [README](four-colours/README.md) |
| *Real quadratic planes that need four colours* (7 pages). χ(ℚ(√d)²) = 4 for d = 11, 23, 35, 59, 71, 95, 119, 131, 155, 179, 191, 239, 251, 263, 359, 431, 443, 455, 491, 599, 611, 791, 851, 911, 935, 959, and 4 ≤ χ(ℚ(√47)²) ≤ 5; a computer proof (SAT with DRAT proofs checked by drat-trim) for the lower bounds, also formally verified in Lean for ten of the fields. | draft, 1 October 2026 | [PDF](quadratic-planes/quadratic-planes.pdf) · [LaTeX](quadratic-planes/quadratic-planes.tex) · [README](quadratic-planes/README.md) |
